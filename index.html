Common questions

Frequently asked.

Do you work on residential and commercial projects?

Yes. Enquiries are welcome for homes, flats, offices, shops, restaurants and other commercial properties.

How are design fees calculated?

Fees depend on property size, project complexity, design scope and required deliverables. Starting prices are indicative only. A personalised quotation will be discussed after reviewing your brief.

Can I request only floor plans?

Yes. You can enquire about residential and commercial floor plans as a standalone service.

How long does a project take?

Timescales depend on project scope, revisions and information available. Your proposed programme will be discussed before work begins.

Do you provide planning and building regulations approval?

Planning and statutory services must be agreed separately and delivered by appropriately qualified and authorised professionals where required. Approval is not guaranteed.

How do I receive a quotation?

Complete the project enquiry form with your requirements, property type, budget and preferred timeline. The studio can then review the brief and discuss a suitable quotation.
