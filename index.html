Frequently asked questions

Before we begin.

Which areas do you serve?

We welcome project enquiries from across the UK. Availability, service scope and any site-visit requirements will be confirmed during the initial discussion.

How much does a design project cost?

Fees depend on the property's size, project complexity and the services and deliverables required. Share your budget and brief so that the scope can be discussed.

Can I enquire about one room only?

Yes. You can submit an enquiry for a single room, an entire home, exterior improvements or 3D visualisation. Suitability depends on the services offered by the studio.

Do you provide planning permission or building regulations services?

These services must be confirmed against the studio's actual qualifications and project scope. Planning approval and building-regulations compliance are not guaranteed. Where required, appropriately qualified UK professionals should be engaged.

How do I get started?

Complete the enquiry form with your location, project type, budget and timeline. Once the form is connected, your submission can be sent to the studio for follow-up.
