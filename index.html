HAVE QUESTIONS?

Yes, we can discuss residential design enquiries across the United Kingdom. Site visits and travel arrangements will be confirmed separately.

Can I start with a £100 consultation?

Yes. The introductory consultation package starts at £100. Its duration and deliverables will be agreed before booking.

No. Planning applications, building regulations, structural calculations and statutory approvals are separate services.
